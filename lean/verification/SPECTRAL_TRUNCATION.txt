1. Rank and Frobenius norm
-------------------------
RankGeometry.lean proves ||H||_F^2 = sum_i eigenvalue_i(H)^2 for real
symmetric H by trace invariance under orthogonal conjugation. The number
of nonzero eigenvalues is rank(H), and each has absolute value <=||H||.
Thus ||H||_F<=sqrt(rank(H))*||H||. All operator norms are Euclidean.

A range-subspace argument proves rank(A-P)<=rank(A)+rank(P). Factoring
the actual representation matrix through its r columns proves rank(P)<=r;
its symmetry is checked directly. Hence rank(A)<=r and symmetric A imply
  ||A-P||_F <= sqrt(2*r)*||A-P||.
FrameGuarantees.lean checks the paper's concrete size substitution:
  ||A-P||<=16*sqrt(n*chi*nu), n>=5120000*r*chi
imply ||A-P||_F<=.01*n*sqrt(nu), for nonnegative chi and nu.

2. Elementary truncation, without a singular-value perturbation input
---------------------------------------------------------------------
DiagonalTruncation.lean first proves that a matrix within operator error e
of diag(d), with every |d_i|>e, has trivial kernel. This follows by applying
the squared Euclidean norm bound to a hypothetical nonzero kernel vector.
Applying this argument to the principal block indexed by |d_i|>e proves
that at most rank(A) such diagonal entries exist. Therefore, retaining the
largest |T| entries with |T|>=rank(A) leaves discarded norm at most
||diag(d)-A||. Equal magnitudes and zero entries are permitted.

SpectralTruncation.lean transfers this bound through orthogonal coordinates.
If Q is orthogonal, ||J-Q diag(d) Q^T||<=zeta, and rank(A)<=|T|, then
  ||J-Q diag(d restricted to T) Q^T|| <= ||J-A||+2*zeta.
The explicit Frobenius stopping criterion implies the needed operator error.
The proof is stronger than the particular symmetric mean application; A
need not be symmetric for this truncation step. The paper now gives the
same elementary argument and explicitly derives the subsequent rank bound.
No algorithm or input-family claim changes.

3. Actual rational representation and largest-entry selection
------------------------------------------------------------
RationalFrame.lean defines frameDiagonal(J,Q)=diag(Q^T J Q) over rationals
and frameRepresentation from selected columns. Exact rational orthogonality
Q^T Q=I implies real unit columns. The retained coefficients are proved to
be exactly their Rayleigh quotients. The sum of rational rank-one terms
(cast to reals) is precisely the selected orthogonal diagonalization.

TopSelection.lean implements merge sorting of all indices by descending
absolute rational diagonal value. It proves length, distinctness and order,
and constructs an injective selection of r indices when r<=n. The largest-
entry property is proved for this actual computed selection, including ties.

FrameGuarantees.lean defines the rational coefficient matrix of the formula
and identifies its cast with the matrix used by the search. Its executable
sortedFrameRepresentation computes the directions and coefficients from Q;
withComputedSpacing subsequently supplies the already verified spacing.
The theorem sorted_frame_approximation_guarantees derives ALL FOUR fields
of the prior ApproximationGuarantees structure. In addition to the original
mean-rank/model/noise/size conditions, it requires only:
  - exact rational orthogonality Q^T Q=I;
  - Frobenius residual <=zeta for diag(Q^T J Q).
The rank correction and truncation quality are conclusions, not separate
numerical hypotheses. This result supplies the input to the previously
verified computed_nodes_on_event and discovered_search_probability theorems.

still needs formal verification. The original asymptotic density/size

hidden-community theorem or add an assumed numerical oracle.

Conventional bit-operation and polynomial-space arguments are not compiler-
