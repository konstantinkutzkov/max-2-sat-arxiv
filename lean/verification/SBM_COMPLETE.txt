Fixed family bounds and the original stochastic block model
============================================================

This batch adds 25 audited mathematical declarations and closes the
mathematical core of thm:sbm. Its status is CHECKED_CORE, like the other
main running-time results. Exactness and the actual search-node bound are
checked; polynomial bit-operation overhead and space remain conventional
paper arguments. The only external mathematical dependencies are the same
two explicitly registered published probability estimates. They remain
hypotheses, not custom axioms or purported formalizations of their proofs.

1. One density constant and a fixed size threshold
---------------------------------------------------
FamilyParameters chooses a positive rational g in
  [1/(512*sqrt(chi)), 1/(256*sqrt(chi))]
and defines
  C0 = 10^9*chi + 1 + (256*r/g)^2.
It proves the existence of one natural N0 at least
  max(100, r, 5120000*r*chi, 1792*r*sqrt(chi)/g).
These depend only on the fixed class parameters. The single hypothesis
  C0*(B^2*log(n)+B0^2+mu^2) <= n*nu,  B>=1
implies every explicit noise-density, diagonal-error, numerical-accuracy
and mean-budget condition used by the earlier concrete algorithm for
n>=N0. The remaining size conditions follow from the choice of N0.
No additional degree, balance, or approximation-quality hypothesis is added.

UniversalNodes proves that the computed directions have squared norm at
most one on every input, including the n<r zero-representation branch.
The exact search's capped-grid and enumeration bounds imply
  nodes <= p_r(n)*2^n,
  p_r(n) = (2*n^3+4*n+3)^r*(2+14*n).
This includes both exhaustive fallbacks and zero coefficient mass. The
useful-event bound is at most p_r(n)*c^n for one 1<=c<2. For n<N0,
  nodes <= 2^N0*p_r(n)*c^n.
Thus the small inputs require only a fixed multiplier, with no algorithm
change. These are actual recursion-node bounds, not an enumeration claim
inferred merely from the number of optima.

FamilyRuntime assembles discovery_probability_from_density and the fully
quantified discovery_family. The latter chooses g, C, K and c before n,
the finite independent-pair law, the original input formula map, and the
hidden mean. For every size, the actual algorithm has
  Pr[nodes <= K*p_r(n)*c^n] >= 1-1/n,  1<=c<2.
All original-model, density and rank conditions are those of the discovery
lemma. No internal numerical, cross-profile, coefficient-budget or size
hypothesis remains. Finite-law exactness was already proved unconditionally.
The convention at n=0 is handled by the deterministic small-input bound.

2. Original SBM law, graph, and formula
---------------------------------------
SBMModel takes an arbitrary group map Fin n -> Fin k and a symmetric real
probability matrix pi with entries in [0,1]. No group balance, nonemptiness,
separation, or probability rationality is required. One Boolean coordinate
per unordered vertex pair decides whether that edge is present. sampledGraph
and sampledFormula construct the graph and its standard Max-Cut encoding;
the actual interaction matrix is exactly -2 times the adjacency matrix.
The previously proved score identity identifies the ordinary cut objective.
Units affect the linear terms only.

The mean matrix is Abar_ij=-2*pi[group i,group j]. It factors through the
k group indicators, proving rank(Abar)<=k. Symmetry, diagonal bounds and
|Abar_ij|<=2 are checked. The latter gives Frobenius norm <=2*n, hence the
same operator-norm bound. The matrix decomposition into this mean, centered
independent entries, and the diagonal correction is proved for the actual
sampled formula, including arbitrary added unit clauses.

SBMVariance defines the minimum and maximum of pi_ab*(1-pi_ab) over ALL
block pairs, exactly as in the paper. Its range bound connects these
extrema to each individual edge variance, even when some groups are empty.
SBMRuntime defines sbmLaw as the original product of Bernoulli laws.
Existing scalar identities give zero centered mean, absolute bound 2, and
variance 4*pi_ab*(1-pi_ab). The parameter substitution is
  r=k, nu=4*v_min, B=B0=mu=2.
For n>=100, log(n)>=1/2, so the paper assumption with C=5*C0 implies
  C0*(4*log(n)+8) <= 4*n*v_min.
The finitely many smaller sizes use the already checked uniform multiplier.

3. Final quantifiers and algorithm inputs
-----------------------------------------
Theorem sbm_family chooses a rational g and constants C,K,c, with
0<C, 0<K and 1<=c<2, BEFORE the number of vertices, partition, block
probabilities, observed graph, or added units are supplied. Under
  v_min>0, v_max<=chi*v_min, n*v_min>=C*log(n),
it proves the original-law probability bound
  Pr[nodes(actual formula-only algorithm) <= K*p_k(n)*c^n] >= 1-1/n.
The hidden partition and probabilities occur only in the sampling law and
proof. diagonalizedNodes/diagonalizedResult receive the formula, k and
fixed rational g, not the group map, probability matrix or mean.

The unit list is quantified as an ARBITRARY FUNCTION of the entire sampled
graph. Thus it may be chosen adversarially after observing the draw; no
independence of the units is assumed. Correctness is unconditional on every
observed formula. sampled_maxcut_correct explicitly gives an optimal cut
when no units are added. The exact finite product law, model identities,
probability bound, fixed constants, and bounded-size cases are all checked.

The theorem is uniform for each fixed partition, which is the conditional
form of the paper's model. A separate theorem mixing random partitions is
not claimed. The main statement defines a fixed partition; its discussion
of a partition drawn first is interpreted conditionally, as stated there.

4. Precise remaining boundary
------------------------------
All four MAIN result families now have checked mathematical content with
conventional arithmetic/space analysis. thm:sbm is conditional only on the
two named published estimates in external-results.json. These were quoted
and their applications checked in earlier batches; no new external theorem
is introduced here.

Two broader appendix results remain PARTIAL in their full stated generality:
- prop:pairmembership: arbitrary non-finite input laws, beyond the proved
  finite-law concentration and membership result;
- cor:planted: the probability-space translation from arbitrary original
  bounded integer-valued inputs to the finite product representation.
Bounded integer ranges are finite, but that general translation has not
been supplied as a Lean proof. The finite-law discovery theorem itself is
now complete, including asymptotic parameters and all sizes. SBM needs
neither unfinished translation, because its ORIGINAL sample space is
already a finite Boolean product law.

The biased-subset appendix remains an unproved target. Bit-operation cost,
polynomial space, and compiler correctness are not machine-cost theorems.
Independent review of paper-to-Lean translation remains distinct from
kernel checking. The correspondence register documents these boundaries.

5. Reproducibility
-------------------
The example suite supplements the universal proofs with present/absent
edges, the actual -2 Max-Cut interaction, exact min/max Bernoulli variance,
an unbalanced assignment with unused communities, and the cut-optimum
connection. Existing numerical and earlier-family checks are retained.
Run python3 verify.py for the full pinned build, fifteen example modules,
public-declaration axiom audit, external-signature audit and paper statement
fingerprints. result.json records the completed outcome.
