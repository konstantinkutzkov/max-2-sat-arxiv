1. All coefficient cases of the useful search
--------------------------------------------
FullSearch.lean proves zero_count_nodes for the original formula without
preprocessing: beta=0 means exactly one guess, zero allowance and the
original gain. The bound is (2+14*n)*c^n. computed_nodes_of_quality combines
this with the positive-mass branch, giving
  computedNodes <= (4*n+3)^r*(2+14*n)*c^n, c<2,
without assuming positive coefficient mass. It requires n>=4 and positive
remainder energy; thus the exhaustive fallbacks are absent on this event.
Their correctness was already checked. This is not a runtime bound on bad
inputs for which the algorithm takes an exhaustive fallback.

RepresentationMatrix.lean defines the actual sum of rational rank-one
terms (cast to real entries) and identifies the matrix in the search gains
with offdiag(J-P), where J is the original formula's gain-coefficient matrix.
No independent matrix is silently substituted for the implemented one.

2. Coefficient budget without eigenvector accuracy
--------------------------------------------------
CoefficientBudget.lean proves |v^T J v|<=||J|| for ||v||_2<=1 using the
Cauchy--Schwarz inequality and the Euclidean operator bound. Therefore
Lambda<=r*||J|| when the retained coefficients are the diagonal Rayleigh
quotients. This needs no eigenvector separation or eigenvector approximation.
The matrix model and ||A||<=mu*n imply
  Lambda <= r*(mu*n+7*sqrt(n*chi*nu)).
Under D>=.9*n*nu, sufficient explicit budgets are
  (256*r*mu)^2 <= g^2*n*nu,
  1792*r*sqrt(chi) <= g*n.
Lean derives Lambda<=g*n*sqrt(D)/64. The paper states these intermediate
budgets and explains their absorption into C(r,chi) and a fixed size cutoff.
The final substitution from the paper's asymptotic parameter statement
is not yet formalized.

3. The raw noise event is now proved
------------------------------------
CrossRows.lean selects distinct unordered-pair coordinates for any row and
any set of earlier columns. The induced law is exactly the corresponding
product law. The variance-sensitive square-sum lower tail applies directly.
For the two prefix rounds it uses exactly B1=floor(n/4), not all earlier
variables in the second round. No assumption of independent row events is
made or required.

RandomEvent.lean proves discovery_scalar_probability and
 discovery_noise_probability for the original finite product input laws.
For n>=20, centered coordinates bounded by B>0, second moments in
[nu,chi*nu], nu>0, chi>=1, and
  10^9*chi*B^2*log(n) <= n*nu,
the event rawGood has probability at least 1-1/n. It comprises:
- raw total energy in [.99*n*(n-1)*nu, 1.01*chi*n*(n-1)*nu];
- each row in B2 has squared mass at least .99*|B1|*nu in B1;
- symmetric matrix norm <=5.8*sqrt(n*chi*nu).
Two energy tails, |B2| row tails and one matrix tail cost at most
(|B2|+3)/n^3<=1/n. The scalar parts are proved internally. The matrix part
has only the two already recorded published-result hypotheses. The energy
normalization is translated explicitly to the Frobenius norm, counting
both matrix orientations of every unordered pair.

4. Conditional end-to-end connection
------------------------------------
DiscoveryBridge.lean defines ApproximationGuarantees, a proposition about
one actual output of a total representation routine. Its four fields are:
- retained direction vectors have norm at most one;
- their coefficients equal the diagonal Rayleigh quotients;
- ||J-P|| <= ||X||+B0+2*zeta;
- ||A-P||_F <= .01*n*sqrt(nu).
These are INTERNAL obligations. They are not declared true, not registered
as published theorems, and not hidden in a custom axiom or an oracle.
The structure does not construct a numerical algorithm or prove existence.

computed_nodes_on_event derives positive remainder energy, normalized
operator bound K=400*chi, corrected row profile, coefficient budget and
then the actual computed-search node bound. Its inputs are the raw event,
these numerical guarantees, the original matrix model/mean bound, n>=100,
0<g<=1/(256*sqrt(chi)), the two explicit mean/size budgets, and
B0,zeta<=sqrt(n*chi*nu). It assumes no row-profile, allowance or final
search-quality conclusion.

discovered_search_probability draws the formula from the original product
law and transfers rawGood to the actual node event, obtaining probability
at least 1-1/n and a base strictly below two. Numerical guarantees need only
hold on positive-mass samples in rawGood. This theorem explicitly retains
those internal guarantees and the two named external matrix estimates.
It is the checked assembly conditional on numerical preprocessing, not a
completed proof of cor:planted or thm:sbm.
