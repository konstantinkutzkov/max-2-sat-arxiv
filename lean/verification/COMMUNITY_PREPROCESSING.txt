1. Exact search, grid spacing, and fallbacks
------------------------------------------
Algorithm.lean defines communitySearch, communityResult, and communityNodes.
The search takes the original formula and a total callback returning rational
coefficients and directions. It runs exhaustive search for n<4 or zero
remainder energy, one zero-adjustment count search for zero coefficient
mass, and the capped projection search otherwise. community_result_correct
proves optimal score and assignment for every well-formed formula and every
such total callback. Approximation quality is not a correctness assumption.

The formal node counter mirrors these branches and counts recursive calls,
including all grid runs, using the earlier proved enumerators. It does not
charge the callback's computation or polynomial arithmetic overhead.
The callback is an explicit interface, not a constructed diagonalization
algorithm. In particular, its totality does not prove that a proposed
numerical implementation terminates or returns an accurate approximation.

RationalRoot.lean implements rootBelow(q) for q>0: if q=u/v, compute
floor(sqrt(u*v))/v using the integer square root. Lean proves positivity
and sqrt(q)/2 <= rootBelow(q) <= sqrt(q). Applying this to
q=g^2*n*D/(64*Lambda)^2 gives exactly the paper spacing interval.
Computed.lean plugs this actual rational computation into the search.
Thus it assumes no exact-real spacing oracle or unspecified dyadic search.

Spacing.lean proves positivity and coverage after replacing delta by
max(delta,n^-2), with at most (2*n^3+3)^r grid tuples on arbitrary n>=1
inputs whose directions have norm at most one. Under the positive budget
Lambda<=g*n*sqrt(D)/64, the computed spacing is at least 1/(2*sqrt(n));
the cap is inactive for n>=2 and there are at most (4*n+3)^r tuples.
The total allowance is <=g*n*sqrt(D)/32.

2. Deterministic quality bounds
------------------------------
Perturbation.lean uses an explicitly defined Frobenius norm and the
Euclidean matrix operator norm. Deleting the diagonal does not increase
the Frobenius norm and increases operator norm by at most a factor two.
For N=X+offdiag(H), the change in Frobenius norm is at most ||H||_F.
The loss of the cross-round row-profile sum is at most sqrt(|B2|)*||H||_F,
by a rowwise triangle inequality and Cauchy--Schwarz. These estimates
require no independence or disjointness of the rows/columns.

Energy.lean proves that raw energy in [.99*n*(n-1)*nu,
1.01*chi*n*(n-1)*nu] and ||H||_F<=.01*n*sqrt(nu) imply
.9*n*nu<=D_N<=1.1*chi*n*nu, for n>=100 and chi>=1.
If each raw cross-round row mass is >=.99*|B1|*nu and each round has
at least n/5 vertices, the corrected profile is >=.07*n*sqrt(n*nu).
This implies the required 4*sqrt(3)*g*n*sqrt(D_N) for
0<g<=1/(256*sqrt(chi)). Rounds.lean proves all floor/cardinality facts
and identifies this profile with the actual twoRound search profile.

Runtime.lean combines these estimates. A bound
||N||<=18*sqrt(n*chi*nu) gives ||N||^2<=400*chi*D_N.
projection_phase_nodes and computed_positive_nodes connect the actual
computed/capped grid, allowance and recursion count to the previously
verified accumulation theorem. Under their explicit quality hypotheses,
they give <=(4*n+3)^r*(2+14*n)*c^n nodes and c<2, with the same two
rounds as the paper. They contain no probability assumption, but they do
assume the deterministic quality conditions that discovery must supply.
The final zero-mass runtime branch and whole-input probability assembly
are not included in this node-bound theorem. Their exactness is checked.

3. Symmetric noise and external results
---------------------------------------
Noise.lean proves X=U+transpose(U) for the actual pair-matrix construction,
hence ||X||<=2*||U||. symmetric_noise_tail applies the earlier checked
upper-triangle threshold 2.90*sqrt(n*chi*nu) to obtain threshold 5.8 and
failure probability <=n^-3, under exactly its finite-law assumptions.
Its only external hypotheses are the already registered Bandeira--van
Handel expectation estimate and Ledoux convex upper tail. No additional
published symmetric-matrix estimate is needed. The paper now follows
this simpler deduction, using the sufficient constant K=400*chi.
The input family and the qualitative fixed-c<2 conclusion are unchanged.

The deterministic remainder_operator_bound checks the operator estimates
from J=A+X-diag(A), a diagonal bound, and the spectral-truncation bound
||J-P||<=||X||+B0+2*zeta. The last bound remains an explicit INTERNAL
obligation here. It is not an external published-result hypothesis.
